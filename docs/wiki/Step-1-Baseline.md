## What was done

The plan came first: a Business Case with scope and measurable success criteria, a Stakeholder Analysis (including the site that is scraped), a Project Plan and six milestones. Each artifact has a review record, the repository got its description and topics, and the milestones became 32 issues.

## What was gained

- Scraping has rules, not only code: the site's `robots.txt` and terms are a stakeholder concern from day one, and they decided that the CSV is never committed.
- Writing the success criteria first (a delay of at least 2 s, no network in tests, no runtime dependency) turned vague wishes into things a test can check.
- One branch and one pull request per milestone keeps each step small and reviewable.
